## Strategic note

Round 2 confirms round 1's finding: the obvious invented-name space is mined, and even real-but-oblique English words are fully occupied in tech/AI. **Vexholm** is the only Low-risk name across 33 screened. Realistic paths: (a) verify Vexholm properly (WHOIS + USPTO TESS + npm/PyPI direct checks, which failed technically in both rounds) and commit; (b) pair a Medium name with a distinctive modifier if needed; (c) run a round 3 with even more abstract constructions (3-letter-friendly, consonant-led) — diminishing returns expected.
